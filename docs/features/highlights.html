How to set it up

Highlights: the best moments on stream

Froggi picks the best moments of each game, set and day by itself and plays them on stream — as video clips from OBS, or redrawn from the Slippi replay without OBS.

How it works:

  • The "Highlights" flow (Stream → Automation → Flows; off until you turn it on) scores every punish and combo: kills count most, plus damage, speed, how much it swung the win chance and doubles multi-kills. Long chases lose points, and moments against CPUs or idle opponents are skipped.
  • Video: OBS's replay buffer saves the moment, and a Media element plays the clips — this game's best 5, the set's best 15, or the day's best.
  • No OBS needed: a Highlight Replay element redraws the same moments from the replay file.
  • The demo overlay "Highlights" shows both. Every rule is a card in the flow — change what counts as a highlight, or ask the AI assistant to.
  • Froggi never deletes your replay or video files.

What are flows?

A flow is a small rule: WHEN something happens, AND some things are true, THEN do something. You build it on a canvas by connecting blocks — like Homey or Home Assistant automations.

  • WHEN — a game starts or ends, a player loses a stock or takes damage, the scene changes, stage striking moves on, a controller combo is held (Windows)
  • AND — singles or doubles, the game mode, a player's stocks or percent, the Froggi scene, the OBS scene, whether the replay buffer is on, or compare any value ("if {{state.players.1.percent}} ≥ 100"). All must be true.
  • THEN — send a web request (smart home, Discord, your server), switch OBS scene, show/hide a source, set a volume, save a replay

Simple Scene and Controller Commands still do one-step jobs — flows are for when you need a condition or something outside OBS.
