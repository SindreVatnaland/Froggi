Realtime overlays

Custom browser-source overlays driven by live Slippi data — scoreboard, HUD, rank preview, win/loss tracker, and more. Build your own layout in the overlay editor.

Tournament ready

Stage striking flow, best-of tracking, and ranked set detection. Score updates automatically as games finish.

Remote stream control

Expose your stream over the internet with Tailscale Funnel. Control OBS scenes, volumes, and overlays from your phone — anywhere.

Live ranked data

Pulls realtime Slippi ranked stats — Elo, placement, wins and losses — and surfaces them on stream automatically.

Dashboard monitoring

Live game dashboard shows stocks, percent, stage, and score at a glance. Preview your OBS output directly inside the app.

No account required

Works entirely offline via Dolphin memory reading. Open source, free forever.
